CHAPTER 01OLGA'S JOURNAL

About me.

Olga Trushkevich
Product & Delivery Leader

I turn complex ideas, ambitious goals and fast-moving teams into digital products that people can understand, use and trust.

For more than ten years, I have worked across product management, project delivery, business analysis and operations. My experience spans AI-enabled products, iGaming, MedTech, EdTech, SaaS, e-commerce and enterprise digitalization — from early discovery and product strategy to launch, scale and continuous improvement.

I am at my best where the path is not yet obvious. I bring structure to uncertainty, translate business needs into practical decisions and create the conditions for specialists, stakeholders and leaders to move in the same direction. I can step back to see the full system, then move close enough to resolve the dependency, requirement or process that is holding delivery back.

“Clarity is not a presentation layer. It is how good products and strong teams move forward.”

My approach is analytical, direct and human. I believe in visible assumptions, honest post-mortems and decisions supported by evidence. I also believe that leadership means helping people grow: explaining the why, making ownership clear and staying calm when priorities shift.

Across international B2B and B2C environments, I have managed up to 12 concurrent projects, led cross-functional teams of up to 70 specialists and worked with budgets of up to $1.5M. The industries change; my focus stays consistent — meaningful outcomes, transparent collaboration and products built around real value.
